Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We've delivered across the GCC, and understand the region's procurement, environmental conditions, and the relationship-first culture of enterprise and government projects.

What happens after you hand the project over?

Every engagement ends with full documentation, Gerber files, and firmware source. If you need continued support after that, our Embedded Engineering Partner retainer covers ongoing work.

What's a typical project timeline?

It varies by project, so we don't quote a generic number upfront. We start by understanding your project overview and requirements, then put together a plan covering the hardware framework and approach. Once that's clear, we give you a concrete timeline built around your actual scope.

Do you sign NDAs before scoping calls?

Yes. We understand the importance of protecting your IP, and sign NDAs to protect both parties before any scoping call.
