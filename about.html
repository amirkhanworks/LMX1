Our Story

Luminox began with Fahad Khan, an electrical engineer who wanted to bring real smart home automation to Indian homes, without the cost and hassle of rewiring a house from scratch. Saad Khan joined early to shape the product's mechanical design. Amir Khan came on board soon after to help take it to market, leading growth, go-to-market, and sales strategy as the product came together. Today, the three of them are focused on scaling both the smart home product and Luminox's growing engineering services work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work with the switches and fans I already have?

Yes. Luminox is a retrofit system, built to work behind the switches, fans, and appliances you already have. Nothing needs to be replaced.

I rent my home. Does this still work for me?

Yes, Luminox is rental-friendly. If you move, we'll relocate your system to your new home once a year at no extra cost.

Are you selling my data?

No. All data is fully encrypted and hosted securely on the cloud, and it stays that way.

Will this last me a lifetime?

Every installation comes with a 2-year warranty, and our team's just a message away for anything after that.

How much of a headache is installation?

Not much of one. It's free, involves no changes to your existing circuits or wiring, and doesn't depend on coordinating with an electrician or anyone else. Our own team handles the whole visit, most homes in under an hour.

What happens when the power goes out?

Like any electrical device, Luminox needs power to run. Automation pauses along with the rest of your home's electronics during a cut, and picks back up automatically the moment power returns.

Can I add more rooms or features later?

Yes. Start with one room, and add more whenever you're ready: a curtain motor, another switch, another room. You're never locked into what you bought on day one.

Can I add accessories like the keychain remote or sensors later?

Yes. Temperature and humidity sensors, the IR blaster, and the keychain remote can all be added to your existing system whenever you're ready.

What if I move homes?

We'll help relocate your system. Renters get this once a year at no extra cost; for other moves, just get in touch and we'll work it out.

How do I get help if something's not working?

Message us on WhatsApp or reach out through the contact details in the footer, and our team will help sort it out.
